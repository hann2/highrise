# Enemy ideas

Started 2026-10-03.

- Leaper: crouches as a clear tell, then pounces in a straight line. You have to dodge it, and then change where you're aiming.
- Tank: charges at you, and stuns itself when it hits a wall, exposing a vulnerable back. Maybe it can smash through a wall.
- Bloater: big and slow; when it bursts, it spawns some number of sprinters.
