# Enemy ideas

Started 2026-10-03.

- Leaper: crouches as a clear tell, then pounces in a straight line. You have to dodge it, and then change where you're aiming.
- Tank: charges at you, and stuns itself when it hits a wall, exposing a vulnerable back. Maybe it can smash through a wall.
- Bloater: big and slow; when it bursts, it spawns some number of sprinters.
- Spider boss: looks and moves kind of like a giant spider, going around the arena and perching in corners, throwing attacks at you. It should also do something to make the battlefield harder to navigate, so it isn't easy to follow around and always be able to shoot.
- Blob boss: a giant softbody blob.
